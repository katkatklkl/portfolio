01 Summary

At a glance

A local volleyball team needed a brand identity that matched how they actually play and carry themselves on court — calm, direct, and hungry. I built the identity from the ground up: positioning, logo, color, typography, and the applied design system across merchandise and team jerseys.

Goal

A cohesive identity that scales from a court bench to a hoodie to a printed jersey.

Approach

Pillars first, visuals second — every design decision laddering back to "calm, direct, hungry."

Deliverables

Logo system, color palette, type system, merch designs, jersey designs.

Outcome

A brand the team can wear, post, and play in — consistent across every touchpoint.
