About

Hi there, I'm Katie!

I'm a Toronto-based UI/UX designer with a background in Game Design. During my years working in the mobile games industry, I've worked on many different things including 2D art, concept work, promotional material, UI design, and user testing.

During my time in games, I found myself really drawn to the UI/UX side of things. I loved digging into where things weren't working for users and finding ways to make the experience better. It felt natural to me and just felt like the right fit, so I decided to transition into UI/UX and make it my focus.

With my background in art, it gave me a strong foundation for the visual craft side of design. And pairing that with user research and identifying opportunities in the experience is what shapes how I approach UI/UX work today.

In my free time I love staying active! I spend most of my time taking dance lessons and playing volleyball. ✨
