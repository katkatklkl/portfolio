01 Summary

At a glance

Presto is the regional transit fare card used across the GTHA. The Add Funds flow on prestocard.ca is a textbook high-stakes, high-frequency task — riders top up specifically because they need to ride now. I evaluated the end-to-end flow against Nielsen's 10 heuristics to find where the experience fails users under time pressure, and where small fixes would meaningfully reduce anxiety.

02 Why this flow

The right flow to evaluate.

The Add Funds flow is the most consequential task on prestocard.ca. Users almost never come to the site to browse — they arrive with intent: my card is empty, I need to top up before tomorrow morning. That makes it the right scope for a heuristic evaluation: high traffic, high anxiety, and bounded enough to redesign within a portfolio timeline.

Why it's worth fixing

  • Failure here doesn't just frustrate — it strands riders mid-commute.
  • The flow combines login, account state, payment, and a hardware-side confirmation — every category of usability concern in one task.
  • The site itself flags a 24-hour delay, which immediately surfaces an expectation-mismatch problem worth investigating.
03 Method

Approach.

I walked through the Add Funds task end-to-end as a registered user on desktop, capturing each screen and noting violations against the 10 heuristics. Each issue was logged with a screenshot, the affected heuristic(s), a rationale, and a severity rating using Nielsen's 0–4 scale.

06 Findings

Issues identified.

Each issue is documented with a screenshot, the affected heuristic, the impact on the user, and a severity rating. Recommendations follow in §08.

ISSUE 01 "Funds may take up to 24 hours to appear" with no progress visibility Sev 4 · Catastrophic
Confirmation screen screenshot
H1 · Status H2 · Real world H9 · Recover

Observation

After paying, users are told the load may take up to 24 hours to appear on the card. There's no live status, no progress indicator, no "ready by" estimate, and no way to verify the load completed before tapping at a gate.

Why it matters

Users come here under time pressure. A 24-hour worst case combined with no feedback turns a paid transaction into an open question — and a possible failed gate the next morning.

Recommendation

Show typical fulfillment time prominently before payment ("Usually ready in ~10 min, up to 24h"). Send confirmation when funds are tap-ready. Provide a status check on the account page.

09 Reflection

What I learned.

Pick a flow with stakes. The Add Funds flow surfaced richer issues than a browse-only task because real money, real time pressure, and a hardware confirmation step compound every small UX failure.

Heuristics are a vocabulary, not a verdict. Tagging issues to H1 / H2 / H9 helps communicate why something feels broken — but the value is in the specific recommendation, not in the heuristic name.

If I had more time… I'd validate severity ratings with a second evaluator and run a small unmoderated test on the confirmation screen redesign.
